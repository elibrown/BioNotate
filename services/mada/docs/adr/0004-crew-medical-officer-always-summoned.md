# Always summon the Crew Medical Officer; no per-step "who performs this" tags

Several Protocols contain Steps a Responder can't safely perform, such as IM and IV drugs, ultrasound, needle decompression and catheterization. We considered tagging each Step with its performer, or flagging only the Protocols that need equipment or medication. Instead, every response starts with two Universal first steps: make the scene safe (including removing the trigger), then call the Crew Medical Officer to the scene now. The Responder keeps following the Steps until the Crew Medical Officer takes over. A Responder never waits for the Crew Medical Officer to start, which matters for CPR, choking and anaphylaxis. The rule is the same for every Protocol, so a new Protocol can't be shipped without it. "Crew Medical Officer" is always written in full in user-facing text.
