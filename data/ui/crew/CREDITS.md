# Crew photo credits

The crew are the four Artemis II astronauts. All photos are NASA photographs,
which are in the public domain in the United States (works of the U.S. federal
government). Copies were taken from Wikimedia Commons, then cropped and resized
for the dashboard.

| A-01-body.jpg, A-02-body.jpg, A-03-body.jpg, A-04-body.jpg (full body) | same order | Artemis II launch-day crew walkout, 1 April 2026 (NASA, NHQ20260401 admin 0004): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Artemis_II_Launch_Crew_Walkout_(NHQ20260401_admin_0004).jpg |

NASA's media guidelines ask that NASA material not be used to imply NASA
endorsement. The health readings and alerts shown for these people in the
dashboard are demo data, not real medical information.
